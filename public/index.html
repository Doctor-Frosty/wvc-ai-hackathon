It's OK to hang up.

Real banks and government offices never mind if you hang up and call them back.

  • Don't give any numbers, buy gift cards, send money, or press keys they tell you to.
  • Call back on a number you look up yourself, from your card or bill. Never use a number they gave you.
  • If they say a family member is in trouble, hang up and call that person directly.
