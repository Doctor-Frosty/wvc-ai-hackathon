Second Look · Check it before you trust it.

Before you do anything, take a second look.

Ask yourself three questions

  1. Did they contact me out of the blue?
  2. Are they rushing me, or asking me to keep it secret?
  3. Do they want gift cards, crypto, a wire transfer, cash, or my private numbers?

If any answer is yes:
Hang up. It's OK.

Careful with friendly strangers

"Hi, how are you doing?" · "Sorry, wrong number!"

Some scams start with kind small talk, then ask for money weeks later. Don't know who it is? Don't reply.
