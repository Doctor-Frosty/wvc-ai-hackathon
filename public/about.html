About Second Look

What it does

When a call, text, email or letter makes you unsure, Second Look gives you a calm second opinion. Paste the message, or type what the caller said, and it tells you:

  • whether it looks like a scam,
  • why, in plain words,
  • what to do now, and who to call.

If you are on a call right now, tap the red button. It's always OK to hang up.

Set up by family

A family member can add the companies you really use and the accounts you really have. Then Second Look knows that a message about a bank or a loan you don't have can't be real.

Your privacy

  • We don't save what you share. There are no accounts.
  • Personal numbers we recognize, like Social Security and bank numbers, are removed on your device before anything is sent.
  • Your family setup stays on this device.
  • To explain its answer, Second Look sends the message, without those numbers, to Google's Gemini AI.

Guidance, not a guarantee

Second Look can't catch every scam. When in doubt, hang up and call back on a number you look up yourself, from your card or a bill.

Not sure what to do? Call the National Elder Fraud Hotline: 1-833-372-8311, or SASCC: (408) 868-1257. For local help any time, dial 211 (free, 24/7). If someone is in danger, call 911.

Who made it
