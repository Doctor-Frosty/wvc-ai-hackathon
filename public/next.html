What's next, with SASCC

Built today and ready to hand over. Here's how it grows.

  1. 🎓

    Practice mode for workshops

    "Spot the Scam": seniors practice with real-looking messages, so they recognize a scam before it ever calls.

  2. 📲

    Easier ways to ask

    Call it on FaceTime (a Chert agent: you call it, it never calls you), forward a text to one number, or hold the space bar and talk.

  3. 🛡️

    Protection everywhere

    A browser extension that flags scam emails and sites as you read, plus guided spam-call blocking and California's DROP request.

  4. 🌐

    More languages

    Spanish, Vietnamese and Chinese, three languages widely spoken in Santa Clara County, with read-aloud in each.

  5. 🤝

    Run by SASCC

    Hosted on SASCC's own accounts, with a clear privacy policy, and the fridge card handed out at every workshop.
