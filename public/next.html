    Practice mode for workshops

    "Spot the Scam": seniors practice with real-looking messages, so they recognize a scam before it ever calls.

    Forward it to Second Look

    Forward a suspicious text or email to one number and get the answer back. No app to open, nothing to type.

    A guided setup for spam-call blocking on their phone and carrier, the Do Not Call Registry, and California's DROP request.

    More languages

    Spanish, Vietnamese and Chinese, three languages widely spoken in Santa Clara County, with read-aloud in each.

  5. 🤝

    Hosted on SASCC's own accounts, with a clear privacy policy, and the fridge card handed out at every workshop.
