## ideas for features/UX

- idea: make a area for family/support to input info and set up for the person. i.e. the family member could input which banks they have accounts with, who they have accounts with, etc. create a simple list that you can compare any suspicious message against
  
  - i.e. if they get an email from Chase Bank, the first thing the tool would do is check against the known list. If the message is about a home loan and they don't even have a home loan, it would automatically mark it as dangerous
    
- idea: build with seniors with mild dementia in mind, so very simple, gentle phrasing, gives all information every time, doesn't assume user knows anything or remembers past experiences/times they used the tool
  
- This would be so that they could just share the entire message, and don't have to figure out what the key info from it is, they could just click `forward` or text a number
